    Instrument
    Sa is set to

    Flute labels vary. Most makers name a flute after Sa (top three holes closed), but some name it after its lowest note (all six closed), so an "E" flute can have its Sa on A. Find my Sa listens to two notes and works out your real Sa, whatever the label says.

    Fine tune 0¢
    Intonation

    Just intonation tunes Re, Ga, Ma, Pa, Dha and Ni as pure ratios from Sa, so they blend with the tanpura. Ga and Ni sit about 14¢ lower than equal temperament.
