local • Nov 3, 2026

build: SD card to Substack photo pipeline

Clearing camera cards meant scrolling Google Photos by hand to see what was already backed up. Built a local index of the library's capture times, matched card files to it by EXIF timestamp, then culled the rest with sharpness scores and perceptual-hash burst groups. Videos are culled from sampled frames, 4K clips are re-encoded to 1080p HEVC on the Mac's media engine, and S-Log2 footage is graded through a Rec.709 LUT computed from Sony's published curve. Before anything is posted, Apple's Vision framework flags frames with people, and RAWs are developed with rawpy and a colour grade per set.
